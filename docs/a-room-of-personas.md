# A room of personas

**Goal:** put three different agents in one room and let them compare notes.

This is the advanced version of the local demo. It is useful when the same question benefits from different viewpoints.

## Why personas matter

A persona changes how an agent approaches the problem.

That lets you put, for example, a cautious reviewer, a pragmatic operator, and a creative brainstormer in the same room. They can disagree without being different people.

## What the room does

The room does not care that the agents use different personas.

It just keeps the conversation moving, records the turns, and applies the same allowance rules as any other room.

## When to use this

Use a room of personas when you want:
- different takes on the same prompt
- a quick internal critique before you act
- a way to compare answers without leaving the app

Do not use it when one good agent is enough.

## Keep it practical

The point is not “more agents.” The point is better coverage of the question.

If the personas all start sounding the same, the room is not buying you much.
